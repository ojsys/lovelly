# M7 — Korram Plus and pay as you go

**Goal (PRD):** screens 12 and 13, filters in the matcher, coin holds and when they are taken
or released, the daily cap and auto top-up. Covers FR-7 (rematch), FR-8, and MN-6 to MN-12.
**Accept:** a filtered match that is skipped within 5 s costs nothing, the cap stops paid
filters but not free matching, and subscribers are never charged coins.

## 1. Filters in the matcher (FR-8)

- **`queue.join {mode, filters, accept_charge}`.** Filters are `gender`
  (`women` | `men`) and `region` (`nearby` | `nigeria` | `africa`). "Everyone" and "Global"
  mean no filter, and those are always free.
- **Who may filter:**
  - Plus subscribers, for free.
  - Pay-as-you-go users, at the price from `POST match/quote`. They must have turned it on.
  - Anyone else trying to filter is refused with `filters_locked`; the app opens `/plus`.
- **Pairing:**
  - Both people's filters must be met: A asking for women only meets a woman, and if she
    filters too, A must meet hers.
  - The gender value is the one from basics (FR-3). Anyone who chose "other" is matched only
    by people with no gender filter.
  - Region (decision 1):
    - **Country** comes from the verified phone number's country code.
    - **Nearby** means the same Nigerian state, from a new State field on the profile.
    - **Africa** means any African country code.
- **After 30 s with no match (FR-9):**
  - Only the free preferences widen (interests, language, nearby). Paid filters stay: the
    user is paying for them.
  - The searching screen then offers **Match free instead** (decision 3).
- **The matcher stays fast:** filter checks are plain comparisons on the ticket, with no
  database calls. The M2 load test (500 users) is run again with 30 % of users filtering.

## 2. Coins for matches (MN-7, MN-8, MN-9, MN-12)

```
queue.join with filters ─► quote (8 gender + 5 region = 13) ─► cap and balance check ─► HOLD
match found ─► the hold moves onto the session
  call or chat runs 5 s ─► TAKE: wallet.post_entry(-13, key match:<session>:<user>)
  either person skips, ends, or is ended for safety before 5 s ─► RELEASE (back in the queue: held again)
leave the queue / disconnect ─► RELEASE
```

- **Holds live in Redis** (PRD: short-lived state), one per user, with an expiry as a safety
  net.
  - **Available coins** = ledger balance − the active hold. Gifts and other holds check
    available coins, not the raw balance.
  - The hold, the check and the take all run under the M6 per-user wallet lock.
- **Taking the coins:** the matcher loop already sweeps once a second, so it takes holds whose
  session has run for 5 s. Each take uses its session as the idempotency key, so a sweep that
  runs twice still charges once.
- **Each person pays for their own filters.** The other person's price doesn't matter.
- **Subscribers (MN-12):** their quote is 0 and nothing is ever held or written. The
  subscription wins even when pay as you go is on too.
- **`POST match/quote {mode, filters}`** returns:
  - `cost` and `free_reason` (`plus` | `no_filters`)
  - `balance`, `available`, `spent_today` and `cap_left`
  - `can_pay`, plus a reason when not: `low_balance` | `cap_reached` | `payg_off`
- **Daily cap (MN-9):** 50, 100, 200 or none; the default is 100.
  - `spent_today` counts all coin spending, gifts included (M6 decision 7).
  - A paid filter needs `spent_today + held + cost ≤ cap`. Otherwise the join is refused with
    `cap_reached`, and free matching still works.
  - The cap only blocks paid filters, never gifts (M6 decision 7).
- **Charge sheet (13, MN-7, MN-11):**
  - Shown before the first paid match of each app session, unless the user ticked "Don't ask
    again this session". A session lasts until the app is closed (decision 8).
  - It shows the price lines, balance before → after, "Spent today N of cap", and the
    auto top-up switch.
  - If coins are short, the main button becomes **Top up coins**. **Match free instead** is
    always there.
- **`SpendSettings`** (PRD model): payg_enabled, daily_cap, auto_topup, topup_pack.
  `GET/PATCH me/spend-settings`. Changing the cap applies at once.

## 3. Rematch (FR-7, MN-6)

- **`POST sessions/{id}/rematch`:** ring someone you skipped (ended with Next) in the last
  24 h. It uses the M5 friend-call ringing:
  - The other person sees "Tobi wants to talk again" and can accept or decline.
  - The call happens in the same mode as before.
- **Price:** 20 coins, held when ringing and taken after 5 s connected, like a filtered
  match. Free for Plus.
- **Refused when:**
  - the person is blocked or reported
  - they're offline or already in a session
  - they skipped you
  - it has been more than 24 h
- **App:** after you tap Next, the searching screen shows "Rematch Tobi · 20 coins" until a
  new match is found (decision 4). Incoming rematches use the incoming-call sheet.

## 4. Korram Plus (MN-6) — screen 12

- **`Subscription`** (PRD fields): user, plan, store, status, current_period_end,
  original_txn_id.
  - **Active** means the status is `active` or `grace` (billing retry) and the period hasn't
    ended.
  - `GET plus/plans` lists the plans; prices come from the store, as for coin packs.
- **Store side:** the M6 store provider is extended with subscriptions.
  - **Google:** `purchases.subscriptionsv2.get`, and real-time notifications for
    purchased, renewed, in grace, on hold, cancelled, expired and revoked.
  - **Apple:** signed transactions, and notifications for subscribed, renewed, failed to
    renew (with grace period), expired, refunded and revoked.
  - Both arrive through the same `purchases/verify` and webhooks, and each store transaction
    is applied once.
  - **Restore** (header of 12) re-sends the store's current subscription to `verify`.
- **Screen 12:**
  - "Subscribe" or "Pay as you go"; Your filters (Show me, Region); the three Plus benefits;
    the plan cards (1 week, 1 month, 12 months); and the CTA "Get Korram Plus · 1 month".
  - "Best value — save N%" is worked out from the store's own yearly and monthly prices.
  - **Pay-as-you-go view:** the price table from the server, the daily cap pills
    (50 / 100 / 200 / None), and "Turn on pay as you go".
  - The filters chosen here are saved on the device and sent with `queue.join`.
- **Home's filter cards** ("Show me · Plus", "Region · Plus") open 12 when locked. Once
  unlocked, they open a small filter picker sheet (no design).

## 5. Auto top-up (MN-10, decision 2)

App-store purchases can't be charged from the server: every purchase needs the user to confirm
in the store's sheet. So the switch can't silently buy coins as MN-10 describes. What's
possible:
- **With auto top-up on:** when a paid match would leave the balance below 50, the app opens
  the store's 550-coin purchase sheet right away. One confirmation, no store screen.
- **True automatic top-up** (saved card) needs the web top-up with Paystack, which is waiting
  on the store-rules review (questions #2).

## Tests and acceptance

- **Backend unit tests (FR and MN IDs in names):**
  - pairing with gender and region filters on both sides, "other" gender, widening that keeps
    paid filters
  - quotes (filters add up, subscriber 0, cap left)
  - hold → take after 5 s, and release for every way a match can end before 5 s
  - take once under a repeated sweep
  - available balance with a hold (a gift can't spend held coins)
  - cap reached refuses paid filters but not a free join
  - subscriber never charged
  - rematch rules and charging
  - subscription states from each store's notifications, applied once
- **Load test:** 500 users, 30 % filtering. The M2 numbers (median wait, no blocked pairs)
  must hold for unfiltered users.
- **Acceptance, live stack (e2e):**
  1. A pay-as-you-go user filters for women and is matched. Skipped at 3 s: the ledger has no
     new row and the hold is gone. Matched again and kept past 5 s: exactly one −8 row.
  2. Cap 50 with 45 spent: a filtered join is refused with `cap_reached`, and the same user's
     free join matches.
  3. A Plus subscriber (fake store) filters and talks past 5 s: no ledger rows.
  4. Store sandbox subscription runs wait for the store accounts (questions #35).
- **App:** widget tests for 12 (both views, plans, cap, restore), 13 (normal, low balance,
  don't ask again, match free), the filter cards and picker, rematch from searching, and the
  incoming rematch. Goldens of 12 and 13 against the designs.

## Decisions to confirm

1. **Regions.** The designs offer Nearby, Nigeria and Africa (and Home says
   "Nigeria · Global").
   - **Recommendation:** country from the verified phone number, and Nearby as the same
     Nigerian state.
   - That needs a **State** field on the profile (the 36 states + FCT; no design). It would
     go under City on 09, and is asked once when someone first picks Nearby.
   - City alone is free text, so it isn't reliable enough for a paid filter.
2. **Auto top-up can't be automatic with app-store billing** (section 5).
   - **Recommendation:** ship the "one confirmation" version now and label it honestly: "Ask
     to add 550 coins when the balance drops below 50".
   - True automatic top-up comes with Paystack. The alternative is hiding the switch until
     then.
3. **Paid filters and the 30 s widening.**
   - **Recommendation:** paid filters never widen (the user is paying for them).
   - After 30 s the searching screen offers "Match free instead" (no design), which releases
     the hold.
4. **Where rematch lives.** No screen shows it; only 12's price table mentions it.
   - **Recommendation:** after Next, the searching screen shows "Rematch <name> · 20 coins"
     for the person just skipped, and it rings them like a friend call.
   - Alternative: leave rematch out of the MVP (and its line out of 12).
5. **Live translation and the ad-free hour are listed in 12 but have no build-plan slot.**
   Translation needs speech-to-text and translation providers; the app shows no ads at all.
   - **Recommendation:** leave both out of M7. Hide "Live translation included" and the two
     price rows until they exist, and keep "no ads".
   - Are ads planned at all? If not, the "Ad-free hour" line goes for good.
6. **Plus products.**
   - **Google:** one subscription, `korram_plus`, with three base plans (weekly, monthly,
     yearly).
   - **Apple:** three products in one subscription group.
   - Plus stays active during the stores' grace period. The products need the same store
     accounts as M6 (questions #35).
7. **Age filter (questions #12).** FR-8 mentions age, but no screen or price does.
   **Recommendation:** not in the MVP.
8. **"This session"** for "Don't ask again this session" means until the app is closed.
9. **Screens with no design:**
   - the filter picker from Home
   - the State picker
   - "Daily cap reached"
   - "Match free instead" while searching
   - the rematch prompt and the incoming rematch sheet
   - the Plus status in Profile ("Korram Plus · renews 12 Nov") and managing the
     subscription (links to the store's subscription page)

## Outcome (done 2026-10-21)

**Acceptance** (`make test-e2e`, live stack, fake store)
- **A filtered match skipped within 5 s costs nothing:** Kwame (pay as you go) filters for
  women and meets Ada. She skips after about 2 s: no ledger row, and his balance stays 100.
  He meets Zainab next and the match runs 6.5 s: exactly one −8 row. Ada pays nothing.
- **The cap stops paid filters but not free matching:** with a 50-coin cap and 50 already
  spent on a gift, the filtered join is refused with `cap_reached`, and the same user's free
  join connects.
- **Subscribers are never charged:** a Plus subscriber (with pay as you go also on) filters
  by gender and region and talks past 5 s; no ledger rows.
- Unit tests cover every way a match can end before 5 s (skip by either person, end, safety,
  disconnect) and a repeated take.
- **Store sandboxes are not run yet:** they still need the store accounts (questions #35).

**Load test:** 500 users, 30 % filtering (as Plus), 3 minutes, 3,324 sessions. Median wait
**1.9 s** (filtered users 2.1 s), p95 6.5 s, **0 blocked pairs, 0 matches breaking a filter,
0 errors**.

**Tests:** backend 270, app 150 (+ goldens of 12 in both views and 13), console 5, end-to-end
12/12 (one failure in seven full runs, in the M2 text-chat test, straight after a load test;
not reproduced in the next six), call harness 9/9. Release APK arm64 **32.8 MB**
(armeabi-v7a 25.6 MB).

**Bugs found on the way (fixed)**
1. **Plus subscribers lost their filters after Next.** Filters were remembered only when
   coins were held, and Plus holds nothing. The load test caught it: 14 matches broke
   someone's filter.
2. **Sessions stranded "active" forever:** a user who disconnects in the instant they're
   matched gets a session created after their disconnect was handled. With both people gone,
   nothing ended it. That keeps the moderation agent in the room and, from M7 on, any coin
   hold waiting.
   - A new orphan sweep in the matcher (about every 30 s) ends sessions older than a minute
     where neither person is online.
   - Live: 76 left by a load run were all ended within 75 s.
3. **The moderation agent wedged under a burst of video calls.** It tried to join hundreds of
   rooms at once and the LiveKit SDK stopped responding, so moderation silently stopped (an
   M4 design gap the load test exposed). Now:
   - it joins at most 4 rooms at a time, each with a 10 s timeout
   - it skips calls that ended while queued
   - after 5 failed joins in a row it exits, and Docker restarts it
   - Straight after a load test it moderated the call test (hidden in 0.48 s) with no manual
     step.
4. **Sheets opened from tab screens sat behind the tab bar:** the charge sheet, the filter
   picker, and M5's friend-request sheet. Their buttons couldn't be tapped. They now open on
   the root navigator.
5. **PATCH requests from the app sent `null` for every unset field**, which the API rejects.
   This broke turning on pay as you go, and M5's "Make main photo" had the same latent bug.
   The Dart client now leaves unset fields out (`include_if_null: false`).
6. **Two quick filter taps kept only the second.** Each tap captured the filters from the
   last build. Now each tap updates one field against the current state.
7. **The load test broke after M4:** the websocket connect limit (30 per minute per IP) is
   now a setting, raised only on `api-load`. Its cleanup no longer tries to delete users that
   gifts or Plus rows refer to.

**Changes beyond the plan**
- **An "Anywhere" region pill on 12 (not in the design),** chosen by default. With only
  Nearby, Nigeria and Africa, everyone on pay as you go would pay 5 coins for a region filter
  by default.
- **Prices from the server:** coin prices per use come in `GET me/spend-settings`
  (`prices`), so 12's table and the rematch prompt don't hard-code them. Plus has its own
  `POST plus/verify`.
- **Enum names pinned** for the new filter fields (as in M6), so existing Dart names didn't
  change.
- The placeholder screen is gone: every screen in the canvas exists.
- **Dev only:** per-IP sign-in limits are configurable (`THROTTLE_OTP_*`) and raised in
  `.env.example`, since full e2e runs onboard many users from one IP. Production defaults are
  unchanged (questions #42).

**Not done / to verify**
- Store sandbox runs for coin packs and Plus (questions #35); naira prices (questions #1).
- **Fully automatic top-up** (Paystack, questions #2), live translation, and the ad-free hour
  (questions #41).
- **Plus status in Profile:** shown on 12 ("You have Korram Plus"), with management linked to
  the store's page. There's no separate status line on 09.
- **Real phones:** no phone run of the new screens (widget tests and goldens only).
