# M4 — Safety

**Goal (PRD):** screen 08, frame sampling to the moderation provider, automatic blur and end
of call, the moderator console, and rate limits. Covers SF-2 to SF-9, plus the "report holds"
on message deletion left over from M2.
**Accept:** a test explicit image injected into a stream is blurred within 2 s, and the report
shows up in the console with its evidence.

## 1. Live video moderation (SF-2, SF-3)

```
video room ─► moderation agent (hidden LiveKit participant, Python rtc SDK)
               │ samples each video track → small JPEG
               │ keeps the last 30 s per session in Redis (evidence ring buffer)
               └► ModerationProvider.scan_frame()  (fake now, real after the bake-off)
                    nudity ≥ threshold  → mute that track in LiveKit (hidden at once)
                                        → moderation.blur to both apps (full blur + "Paused for safety")
                                        → end session (reason moderation) → moderation.ended
                                        → automatic Report (severity: nudity) with evidence
                    estimated age < 18  → suspend the account until a human reviews it (SF-3)
```

- **New Compose service `moderator`.** The matcher tells it when a video session starts or
  upgrades (Redis pub/sub). It joins as a **hidden** participant, using a token with the
  `hidden` grant: subscribe-only, never published, invisible to the users.
- **Why a server-side agent:**
  - It meets the PRD ("sample … on the server").
  - It gets frames directly, with no client trust and lower latency than LiveKit Egress
    snapshots to storage.
  - The cost is one connection plus frame decoding per active video call. It scales by
    running more agent processes, each owning a share of the rooms.
- **No audio moderation** (after the MVP in the PRD).

## 2. Reports and blocks (SF-4, SF-5) — screen 08

- **`POST reports {session_id, reason, block}`.**
  - Reasons from the design: `nudity`, `harassment`, `minor`, `scam`, `fake`, `other`.
  - Severity follows SF-6: possible minor > nudity > harassment > scam > other. `fake`
    counts as `other`.
  - The server copies the last 30 s of sampled frames (video calls) and the chat transcript
    (all sessions) into the private `evidence` bucket, records `evidence_uri`, ends the
    session, and blocks the peer if `block` is set.
  - The reported person is never told who reported them.
  - Rate limit: 10 reports per hour per user.
- **App:**
  - The report sheet opens over the paused call: remote video hidden, both sides' media
    paused on this device.
  - Six reason options, "Also block <name>" (on by default, as in the design), and Cancel,
    which resumes the call.
  - On submit: "Thanks for keeping Korram safe", then **Meet someone new** or **Back to
    home**.
- **Report in text chat:** the Chat design (06) has no report button, but the app stores
  require report and block in every chat (NFR). A shield button in the chat header, like the
  call screens, opens the same sheet.
- **Evidence retention:** 30 days after the report closes; reports escalated or held for
  SF-9 are kept.
- **Message deletion:** the 7-day purge of stranger messages now skips sessions with an open
  or escalated report.

## 3. Moderator console (SF-6) — `admin/` + `/admin-api/`

- **Login:** staff accounts only (Django users with `is_staff` in the **Moderators** group),
  phone and password, JWT. Other users get 403.
- **Report queue:** open reports sorted by severity, then by age, with counts per severity
  and a filter for automatic (agent) vs user reports.
- **Report detail:**
  - Evidence frames as a strip, through 5-minute signed URLs.
  - The chat transcript.
  - Both people's summaries: first name, age, city, verified, risk score, earlier reports and
    actions. Never the date of birth.
- **Actions:** **warn**, **suspend 24 h**, **suspend 7 d**, **ban**, **dismiss**,
  **escalate**.
  - Every action writes a `ModerationAction` row. The table is append-only, enforced by a
    Postgres trigger that rejects UPDATE and DELETE.
  - A suspension or ban ends any live session (`moderation`) and closes the user's
    websockets.
  - A ban deny-lists the phone hash and the user's device ids.
- **Verification queue:** the selfie manual review from M1 moves here from Django admin
  (approve or reject, with the selfie frames).
- **SF-9:** "Mark as suspected child abuse" puts the report on legal hold. Its evidence can't
  be deleted (trigger plus a storage check) and stays out of the purge; the console records
  the referral to the authorities (date, agency, reference).

## 4. Enforcement in the app

- **`GET me/status`** returns `{status, suspended_until, notices[]}`. A suspended user
  sees an "Account suspended until …" screen (no design). A warning appears once on next
  open as a dialog (no design).
- **Device identifier for bans:** the Android ID (stable per app-signing key, survives a
  reinstall) or the iOS identifierForVendor. It's sent as `X-Device-Id` and stored on the
  user. Sign-up and sign-in from a banned device are refused.

## 5. Rate limits (NFR)

Per user, Redis-backed:
- reports: 10/h
- queue joins: 120/h
- websocket connects: 30/min per IP
- media-token refresh: 30/h
- chat: already 20 per 10 s

Auth limits are from M1. Every limit returns `429` with `retry_after`.

## Tests and acceptance

- **Backend unit tests:**
  - severity ordering
  - evidence capture: frames + transcript, signed URLs
  - the SF-4 retention purge and legal hold
  - append-only triggers on `ModerationAction`
  - each action's effects (session ended, sockets closed, phone and device denied)
  - suspended users can't match
  - the rate limits
  - message deletion respecting report holds
- **Agent tests:** fake provider, sampled frames → blur/end/report decisions; age estimate
  → suspend.
- **Acceptance harness** (extends `make test-calls`):
  - A participant switches its camera to a **test image the fake provider flags**: a magenta
    frame with an embedded marker. No real explicit imagery is used in tests.
  - The harness measures from injection until the other participant sees the track muted.
    **Pass: ≤ 2 s.**
  - It then checks, through `/admin-api/`, that the automatic report is in the queue with
    frames and transcript.
  - A user report from the other bot shows up the same way.
- **Console:** Vitest component tests (queue sorting, actions, evidence viewer) and an API
  contract test.
- **App:** widget tests for the report sheet (both outcomes), the chat report button, the
  "Paused for safety" blur, the moderation-ended message, and the suspended screen; golden
  of 08.

## Decisions to confirm

1. **Sampling rate.** SF-2 says one frame every 2 s, but the acceptance asks for a blur within
   2 s. At 2 s sampling the worst case is about 2 s plus scan time, so some injections would
   miss. **Recommendation:** sample every 1 s for the first 30 s of each video stream (when
   most abuse happens in random chat) and every 2 s after. The 2 s acceptance is then met
   where it matters, at about 1.3× the scan cost of a flat 2 s. The alternative is a flat 1 s
   (double the cost) or a flat 2 s (acceptance relaxed to "within 2 s of sampling").
2. **Moderation provider** (questions.md #3). M4 ships the full pipeline with the fake;
   picking the real one is the bake-off (accuracy on darker skin tones, cost per check,
   latency from Lagos). Candidates: AWS Rekognition (DetectModerationLabels), Google Cloud
   Vision SafeSearch, Hive, Sightengine. The adapter takes about a day once chosen.
3. **"Blur the stream at once."** Our servers can't blur WebRTC video in flight, so the agent
   **mutes the track in LiveKit**: the viewer stops receiving it within milliseconds. The
   apps also blur fully and show "Paused for safety". Then the call ends.
4. **Moderator login:** password only for the MVP, staff accounts created by an admin. Add
   two-factor before launch? (Recommended, not in the PRD.)
5. **Device bans** use the Android ID or identifierForVendor. They're defeated by factory
   resets and new devices, but stop casual repeat accounts. OK?
6. **SF-9 referral process.** The console records the referral, but who reports, to which
   Nigerian authority (and whether to NCMEC), and how fast is a legal decision. **Please
   confirm with counsel.** The technical hold is in place either way.
7. **Screens with no design:** report button in text chat, "Paused for safety", "This call
   was ended for safety reasons", "Account suspended", the warning dialog, and the whole
   moderator console. The console will be plain and functional, using the app's colours.
